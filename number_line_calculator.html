Number Line Calculator

Type a sum or difference of whole numbers. Each number after the first is broken into rows and columns of the table, and the answer is found by moving through the table from the first number. Separate calculations with commas to draw letters next to each other, one blank column apart.

On the table

Every cell visited is highlighted, including the ones crossed between landings. The superscript gives the order of each visit. Green is the starting number, red is the result. Letters sit one blank column apart; when the next letter will not fit in the columns left, it goes on a new copy of the table to the right.
